About me - Chapter 3

Boundless Opportunities (or so I thought)

The big next step: finding a job. Anyone, graduates especially, in the job market 2024-2025 can attest how hard it is to be considered, let alone, successfully secure a position. It took me 6 months of hard work- re-building my portfolio, attending events, marketing myself, coding challenges, interview prep and Linkedin optimisation, to land the intern role at Boundless.

This opportunity was literally heaven-sent. No complicated 15-step application process, 2 round of interviews & a take-home coding challenge, all within a week. I could not believe my eyes when the offer email came through the next Tuesday morning. I cried honest tears. Getting this offer pulled me out of the imposter syndrome well I'd slowly been drowning in, rejection email after rejection email.

I am so incredibly thankful for the opportunity. I've learned so much about working in a team, working independently, working from home and above all, the Boundless way-of work and what they stand for: "Growing through experimentation, overcoming new challenges with optimism and an open mind".

10/06/2026

Welp, this all came crashing down mid January 2026. We were notified in a last minute townhall that Payoneer, a big Israeli-American payments company headquartered in the USA, were acquiring Boundless, effective by the end of that month.
Everyone was nervous, and that did not die down as we met the team we were supposedly "working with" in the foreseeable future. Very different way of work, and a timeline for migration so sped up it could only mean one thing: layoffs.

And well, just 2 months later, my contract was not renewed and 80% of the team was let go. It was kind of brutal. I was in denial for a while, I tried to be understanding of the founders and the pressure they were in, trying to secure the future of the company. But at the expense of the team? Some of my coworkers have families to feed (I guess that's where employment laws kick in).
However, I was salty (still kind of am). I was at the beginning of my career, I gave my all, and I really saw myself growing with Boundless.
